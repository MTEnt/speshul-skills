# Creative interview

Ask only unresolved questions. Prefer one compact question per turn. Stop when remaining choices can safely become documented defaults.

## Gate 1: desired action

What should the audience do after seeing the post?

- understand or remember something
- visit a link
- register or buy
- reply or participate
- contact the Page

Record one primary CTA.

## Gate 2: audience

Who is this specifically for, and what do they already know or believe?

Record:

- audience segment
- problem, desire, or context
- familiarity with the brand or topic
- language and locale

## Gate 3: offer and evidence

What is being offered or claimed?

Record:

- exact offer
- deadline or availability
- proof supplied by the user
- claims requiring research
- forbidden or regulated claims

## Gate 4: format

Which deliverable best serves the objective?

- text or link
- single image
- product image or carousel
- general or cinematic video
- ad or UGC video
- narrated explainer

Do not default to video when a simpler format communicates better.

## Gate 5: continuity and identity

What must remain identical across generated assets?

- real person
- fictional character or mascot
- product or packaging
- location
- wardrobe and props
- style and palette

For a real person, ask explicitly:

> Has this person consented to AI training or synthetic depiction for this campaign, and do you have the right to use all reference photos?

Stop if the answer is not an unambiguous yes.

## Gate 6: publication

Resolve:

- Facebook Page name and numeric ID
- publish now, schedule, or draft only
- deadline and timezone
- organic post or paid creative
- AI disclosure or human-review requirement
