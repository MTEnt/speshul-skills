# Facebook content strategy

Current-source review: 2026-08-07.

## Evidence-backed principles

Meta says organic Page reach can vary with prior engagement, engagement with similar content, content quality, time of day, and device context. These are explanatory factors, not a deterministic recipe. Never promise that a specific time, format, caption length, or hashtag will increase reach.

Meta's current awareness-ad guidance recommends:

- communicate quickly and use the first few seconds well
- show brand or product imagery early when relevant
- tell a cohesive, concise story
- make video understandable and engaging with sound off
- tailor variants to meaningful audience segments
- make the desired action easy to understand

These are paid-creative recommendations. They are useful production heuristics for organic work, but they are not proof of organic ranking behavior.

Facebook supports captions and custom thumbnails for Page video. Captions improve accessibility and preserve meaning when viewers watch without sound.

## Planning rule

Choose one primary objective:

| Objective | Useful content shape | Primary success signal |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | short story, reveal, useful fact, behind-the-scenes | qualified reach or video retention |
| Education | demonstration, explainer, checklist, myth correction | completion, saves, meaningful comments |
| Consideration | comparison, case example, process, FAQ | link clicks or qualified replies |
| Conversion | specific offer, deadline, proof, clear next step | completed target action |
| Community | question, spotlight, response, participation prompt | substantive comments and contributions |

Do not optimize all five in one post.

## Copy architecture

Use only the parts the brief needs:

1. Opening: the audience-relevant tension, outcome, or concrete detail.
2. Value: the useful fact, story, demonstration, or offer.
3. Proof: evidence, source, example, or clearly labeled opinion.
4. Action: one primary CTA.

Avoid unsupported urgency, invented testimonials, fake scarcity, engagement bait, and claims that the creative itself cannot substantiate.

## Video architecture

- Opening frame must communicate before audio starts.
- Each shot has one clear subject and one primary action.
- Captions or on-screen text must stay within safe visual areas and remain legible on a phone.
- Brand presence should be natural and visible early when brand recognition matters.
- A thumbnail is a separate deliverable, not an afterthought.
- A finished video needs a transcript and disclosure review even when captions are burned in.

## Feedback loop

Record the campaign objective, audience, creative hypothesis, post ID, publication time, content hash, and observed result. Change one meaningful variable at a time when comparing variants. Virality or attention scores are comparative evidence, not outcome guarantees.
