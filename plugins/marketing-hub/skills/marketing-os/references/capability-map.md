# Capability map

Every skill in the hub answers one question. Route by the question, not by the channel the user named.

## Evidence

| Skill | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `$truth-file` | Create or update the shared marketing truth file: product truth, audience and buying situation, alternatives, positioning inputs, proof inventory, objections, voice rules, claim limits, and measurement baseline, each labeled by evidence state. |
| `$customer-evidence` | Plan, run, or synthesize customer research: interviews, surveys, call and support transcripts, reviews, public communities, jobs-to-be-done, segments, a customer-language library, and evidence-grounded personas. |
| `$alternatives-map` | Research the alternatives customers can actually choose and write about them honestly: source-backed competitor dossiers, market landscapes, and the comparison, versus, alternatives-to, best-for, and migration pages built on them. |

## Decision

| Skill | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `$constraint-diagnosis` | Locate the weakest evidenced transition in the customer journey before any tactic is chosen: audience access, qualification, comprehension, credibility, friction, activation, retention, economics, or measurement. |
| `$growth-plan` | Build a coordinated marketing, growth, go-to-market, fractional-CMO, 90-day, or annual plan: diagnosis summary, strategic thesis and its sacrifice, initiatives with owners and budgets, team and partner model, measurement, dependencies, stop rules, and operating cadence. |
| `$positioning-and-offer` | Decide how the product is framed and what exactly is exchanged: category and competitive frame, differentiated capability and reason to believe, the qualifying customer, and the complete offer with scope, delivery, price structure, guarantees, bonuses, risk allocation, and truthful urgency. |
| `$pricing-and-packaging-and-packaging` | Research, design, audit, or change price levels, value metrics, tiers, freemium and trial structure, usage and subscription models, discounts, price increases, and pricing pages. |
| `$tactic-portfolio` | Generate, compare, and rank growth mechanisms into a small evidence-backed portfolio with idea cards: mechanism, prerequisites, first artifact, cost boundary, time to signal, measure, failure mode, and stop or scale rule. |
| `$decision-lenses` | Examine one marketing decision through three to five documented strategic lenses that genuinely conflict, collect independent positions and rebuttals, research the facts that could settle the disagreement, and chair a synthesis. |
| `$behavior-design` | Explain a customer behavior or improve a choice environment with behavioral science: attention and comprehension, motivation and progress, perceived risk, choice architecture, social information, memory and brand, pricing perception, and diffusion. |

## Routing rules

- A request that names a channel usually needs a decision first: run `$constraint-diagnosis` unless the constraint is already evidenced.
- A request for copy, an asset, or a page goes to the Make or Convert owner directly when the truth file already answers audience, offer, proof, and voice.
- Measure skills run before optimization claims; Operate skills run after the deliverable exists.
- Do not chain more than three specialists for one deliverable without returning to the user with the decision record.

## Coverage note

Coverage means each row has a discoverable trigger, an operating contract, an output contract, and a truth or safety boundary. It does not mean copied wording, a frozen platform specification, named-expert impersonation, or permission to publish, spend, contact people, or alter external accounts.
