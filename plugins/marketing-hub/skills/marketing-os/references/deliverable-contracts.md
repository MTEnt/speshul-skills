# Deliverable contracts

Choose the smallest contract that matches the request. Omit fields that do not affect the decision; do not return empty ceremony.

## Research brief

- decision to inform;
- scope, audience, market, period, and exclusions;
- source plan and evidence standard;
- findings with sources and counterevidence;
- unknowns and confidence limits;
- decision and next validation step.

## Positioning and offer

- selected audience and buying situation;
- alternatives and current behavior;
- differentiated capability and resulting value;
- category or frame;
- offer, terms, prerequisites, exclusions, and risk treatment;
- proof inventory and claim limits;
- rejected directions and why.

## Marketing plan

- outcome, baseline, horizon, and constraint;
- factual foundation and assumptions;
- audience, positioning, offer, and route-to-market thesis;
- prioritized now/next/later work;
- artifact, owner, dependency, cost or capacity boundary;
- metric, threshold, review date or condition;
- risks, deferred options, and stop rules.

## Campaign brief

- outcome and audience state;
- intended belief or behavior change;
- promise, mechanism, proof, offer, CTA;
- channel role and asset map;
- creative territories and selection rationale;
- production, rights, approval, timing, and budget constraints;
- measurement and stop/continue rule.

## Conversion review

- inspected surface, path, segments, and evidence;
- observed friction or failure, separated from causal hypothesis;
- prioritized changes by expected impact, evidence, effort, and risk;
- proposed copy or interface artifact when requested;
- instrumentation and experiment or monitoring plan;
- inaccessible or unverified behavior.

## Content or channel system

- channel job and audience state;
- themes based on customer need and product evidence;
- source-to-asset plan and distribution method;
- publishing capacity and cadence rationale;
- CTA and handoff path;
- measurement, feedback loop, reuse, and retirement rule.

## Performance readout

- decision and recommendation first;
- metric definitions, baseline, period, and data quality;
- result, cost, guardrails, and segment differences;
- causal limits and alternative explanations;
- next action, owner, and review condition.
