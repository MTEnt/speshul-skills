# Mobile messaging system

## Compliance first

Record jurisdiction, brand and sender identity, number or sender type, registration, consent source, exact disclosure, message categories, frequency, charges disclosure where applicable, age or sensitive-category limits, quiet hours, HELP and STOP behavior, confirmation, evidence retention, re-consent, and data deletion.

Requirements vary by country, carrier, provider, and transactional versus promotional purpose. Use current authoritative guidance and legal review where risk warrants it.

## Sender and platform choice

Compare long code or registered application-to-person routes, toll-free, short code, alphanumeric sender where available, and provider-managed options by geography, throughput, trust, registration, reply behavior, cost, portability, deliverability, MMS, support, and compliance. Verify current availability.

## When SMS fits

Requested verification, transaction or service update, appointment reminder, time-sensitive account action, consented promotion, replenishment, abandoned action, or live support may justify SMS. Use another channel for long explanation, low urgency, or repeated education.

## Message writing

Identify the sender when not obvious, state relevant context, name the action, use a recognizable secure destination, include only real deadlines, and place required opt-out language appropriately. Keep dynamic data bounded and safe.

For MMS, verify current file type, dimensions, size, rights, accessibility alternative, and text fallback.

## Sequence types

Welcome and confirmation, abandoned action, appointment, delivery or status, post-purchase, support, replenishment, limited promotion with real terms, renewal, payment action, and win-back. Coordinate with email and product journeys to prevent collisions.

## QA and monitoring

Test consent capture, registration, sender, keyword responses, suppression, quiet hours, localization, variables, links, carrier filtering, delivery receipts, duplicate triggers, provider retries, number recycling, replies, support routing, outage, and fallback.

Monitor delivery, failure, opt-out, complaint, conversion, retained value, frequency per person, cost, and support. High delivery does not prove welcome communication.

## Output

Return consent and sender requirements, flow diagram, complete messages, timing and suppression, provider-neutral event contract, platform comparison, QA, monitoring, and activation approval.
