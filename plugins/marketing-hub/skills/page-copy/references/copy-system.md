# Copy system

## Brief

Capture audience and situation, awareness and decision stage, relevant alternative, desired progress, current pain or cost, offer, mechanism, product truth, proof, objections, risk, primary action, next state, voice, channel, and forbidden claims.

If positioning or offer choices conflict, surface the decision rather than resolving it invisibly in prose.

## Message hierarchy

1. Make the intended person and useful outcome recognizable.
2. Explain what is offered in concrete language.
3. Show the mechanism or meaningful difference.
4. Demonstrate value with proximate evidence.
5. Address the risks that block the next action.
6. State commitment, action, and what follows.

## Copy frameworks

Select by decision job:

- situation -> problem -> consequence -> mechanism -> action;
- desired progress -> obstacle -> differentiated path -> proof -> action;
- alternative -> limitation -> new criterion -> product fit -> action;
- claim -> evidence -> explanation -> implication;
- before state -> transition -> after state, only with verified proof;
- objection -> underlying risk -> truthful answer -> evidence -> choice.

Frameworks organize reasoning; do not force every sentence into a formula.

## Page patterns

- **Homepage:** audience and category, core value, mechanism, proof, use cases or paths, objections, commercial next step.
- **Landing page:** source-message match, one promise, relevant mechanism, proof, offer, objections, focused action.
- **Pricing page:** value metric, plan fit, limits, total commitment, comparison, proof, terms, recommendation basis.
- **Feature page:** user problem, workflow, capability, result, evidence, limitations, related product path.
- **Product page:** decision context, concrete product behavior, fit, proof, price or access, next step.
- **About page:** why the organization is credible and relevant, not an unsupported mythology.

Use only sections the decision requires.

## Headline and CTA

A headline may lead with audience progress, costly situation, distinct mechanism, category clarity, or a credible point of view. The subhead completes missing product, audience, or mechanism information.

CTA copy names the real action and expected next state: view the live workflow, compare plans, calculate the estimate, book a qualification call, start a trial, or download the template. Avoid promising a result the click cannot deliver.

## Natural transitions

Move because the reader's next question changes: from outcome to explanation, mechanism to evidence, evidence to fit, fit to objection, and objection to action. Use headings and logical continuity instead of filler such as empty scene-setting or repeated summary sentences.

## Voice

Preserve characteristic vocabulary, sentence length, directness, humor, formality, and prohibited tendencies from verified examples. Do not normalize distinctive writing into generic corporate prose.

## Output and QA

Return ready-to-place copy with meaningful section labels and necessary state copy. A five-second scan should reveal who, what, value, and action. Every claim must be supported, attributed, qualified, or removed; a competitor should not be able to reuse the page by swapping the logo.
