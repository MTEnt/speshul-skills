# Event system

## Portfolio decision

Choose host, sponsor or exhibit, speak, attend, partner, or skip. Compare audience fit, relationship access, customer value, credibility, timing, total travel and production cost, staffing, lead or participant rights, content opportunity, historical evidence, and next-best use of resources.

## Universal arc

### Before

Define premise, audience, goals, invitations or meeting outreach, registration, qualification, reminders, speaker and asset preparation, accessibility, consent, tracking, sales and support coordination, and contingency.

### During

Deliver useful content or interaction, capture permitted data, moderate, support participants, document questions and evidence, manage incidents, confirm next steps, and protect recording rights.

### After

Segment follow-up by actual state, deliver promised material, route support and sales work, publish only approved recordings or assets, repurpose useful content, reconcile data, evaluate outcomes, and document learning.

## Webinars and owned events

Specify learning outcome, format, speaker credibility, registration fields, reminders, calendar behavior, platform and backup, moderation, captions, recording consent, CTA, replay access, follow-up, and no-show path. The content should remain useful without the pitch.

## Sponsorship and booth

Verify attendee evidence, package rights, speaking or meeting access, booth location and constraints, branding, lead capture and consent, list use, organizer support, cancellation, total cost, staffing, shipping, demo, and historical quality. Define pre-booked meetings, onsite behavior, qualification, handoff, and reconciliation.

## Attending

Set target conversations and sessions, research participants where permitted, create useful reasons to meet, coordinate team notes, and follow up with context. Avoid indiscriminate badge collection or automated outreach.

## Speaking

Develop audience-specific thesis, evidence, structure, demonstrations, disclosure, accessible slides, rehearsal, host requirements, recording and reuse rights, Q&A boundaries, and follow-up asset. Do not turn the talk into a disguised product pitch.

## Content engine

Plan source capture, permissions, live notes, short clips, transcript, article, visual summary, sales or product learning, and replay. Reuse only material that retains context and rights.

## Measurement

Use qualified attendance, show rate, meaningful participation, meetings held, opportunity progression, customer outcomes, content use, partner value, total cost, staff time, support burden, and corrected attribution. Define review window based on the sales or adoption cycle.

## Output

Return role and portfolio decision, audience value, budget, run-of-show, assets, responsibilities, rights and compliance, lead handling, follow-up, measurement, cancellation, and incident plan.
