# Prospecting system

## Branches

### B2B accounts

Use organization, industry, geography, size only when relevant, workflow, technology, regulation, buying group, operational evidence, and disqualifiers. Separate account from contact.

### SaaS design partners and early users

Look for acute problem, current workaround, tolerance for product immaturity, workflow access, feedback capacity, decision authority, and mutual expectations. Do not disguise customer discovery as a mature-product sale.

### Local businesses

Verify category, operating status, service area, location, current digital or operational evidence, owner or decision route, local regulation, and actual need. Avoid generic lists of every nearby business.

### Existing-account expansion

Use permitted product use, account structure, realized value, unmet need, contract, customer-success ownership, and consent. Coordinate with the accountable customer team.

## Data sources

Use official sites, registries, professional profiles, directories, public filings, job listings, technology observations, event lists, product or integration evidence, local listings, user-provided CRM data, and authorized providers. Follow terms, robots, access, privacy, and contractual boundaries.

## Demand signals

Possible signals include relevant hiring, product or geographic expansion, technology migration, regulation, funding only when it changes need or capacity, public initiative, leadership change, support or review language, active evaluation, product behavior, event participation, or explicit request. Record the exact signal and date. Do not infer private intent.

## Qualification

Evaluate separately:

- fit: stable characteristics and problem relevance;
- signal: observed evidence suggesting timing;
- access: verified route to a relevant role;
- value: plausible benefit and economic importance;
- risk: disqualifier, stale data, conflict, compliance, or delivery mismatch.

Show any weights as judgment and retain underlying facts. Missing data is not positive evidence.

## Contact data

Collect only fields needed for the authorized use. Track role, route, source, checked date, confidence, suppression, and provider if applicable. Validate provider claims and do not expose personal data beyond need.

## Output

```text
account,domain,location,fit_evidence,signal_evidence,role,contact_name,contact_route,source_urls,checked_date,confidence,disqualifiers,priority,next_research
```

For a small list, a readable table is sufficient. Report method, coverage, invalid and excluded records, freshness, compliance, capacity, and next verification.
