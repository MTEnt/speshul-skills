# Marketing tool registry

This is a capability index, not an endorsement or current-availability claim. The examples were reviewed as candidate categories on 2026-08-26; verify every selected tool against current official documentation and the user's actual stack.

## Analytics and product behavior

GA4, Adobe Analytics, Plausible, Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog, Pendo, Segment, Supermetrics, Coupler, Hotjar, and Optimizely may cover web analytics, event analysis, session evidence, experimentation, customer-data routing, and reporting aggregation.

Choose from required identity, event depth, privacy, warehouse access, retention, experimentation, current connectors, and operating ownership. Do not install overlapping analytics systems without a decision they uniquely support.

## Search, content, and competitive research

Google Search Console, Semrush, Ahrefs, DataForSEO, Keywords Everywhere, Similarweb, G2, Trustpilot, Exa, Firecrawl, Browserbase, SparkToro, RankParse, and specialized rank or backlink services may support search performance, crawling, competitive evidence, reviews, audience research, and permitted retrieval.

Prefer authoritative first-party search data for owned properties. Treat third-party traffic, rank, audience, and technology estimates as modeled evidence with dates and methods.

## Advertising

Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, and other current platform APIs or exports may support campaigns, reporting, audiences, creative, and conversion uploads. Use the platform's official API, SDK, connector, or an existing authorized MCP when available.

Never assume a reporting token permits campaign mutation. Separate reporting, asset upload, draft creation, publishing, budget, billing, and audience permissions.

## CRM, sales, and scheduling

HubSpot, Salesforce, Close, Outreach, Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clay, Clearbit, Hunter, Snov, Truelist, Gong, Calendly, SavvyCal, AudienceTap, Cogny, Converly, RB2B, and authorized professional or registry sources may support accounts, contacts, enrichment, engagement, calls, pipeline, and scheduling.

Verify contact-data rights, jurisdiction, suppression, field ownership, record matching, merge authority, and vendor provenance. Do not turn estimated intent into a verified fact.

## Email, messaging, and push

Customer.io, Mailchimp, SendGrid, Resend, Postmark, Kit, Beehiiv, Klaviyo, Brevo, ActiveCampaign, Instantly, Lemlist, NitroSend, Sequenzy, Twilio, Plivo, Postscript, Attentive, OneSignal, and other providers may support lifecycle email, transactional messages, outbound sequences, SMS, MMS, and push.

Match the tool to permission model, transactional versus marketing separation, sender requirements, deliverability, geography, volume, event triggers, suppression, replies, and audit evidence.

## Billing, subscriptions, referrals, and partners

Stripe, Paddle, Rewardful, Tolt, Mention Me, PartnerStack, Dub.co, Crossbeam, Introw, and related systems may support payments, subscriptions, commissions, links, partner overlaps, referrals, and deal registration.

Treat billing and reward state as financial data. Reconcile refunds, reversals, duplicate webhooks, currency, tax, attribution, and ledger ownership before automating payouts or entitlements.

## Social, community, and publishing

Buffer, Firehose-style social-data services, and native platform tools may support approved monitoring, scheduling, and reporting. WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Contentful, Strapi, Shopify, GitHub-backed content, and other CMS or commerce systems may own content, media, products, and publication state.

Preserve draft, review, localization, version, rights, preview, schedule, publish, rollback, redirect, and cache behavior. Do not publish by treating write access as editorial approval.

## Events, forms, support, and automation

Typeform, Intercom, Demio, Livestorm, Wistia, Zapier, and current webinar, form, support, or automation systems may support registration, customer messaging, events, media, and cross-system flows.

Verify participant consent, replay rights, field schemas, webhook ordering, duplicate delivery, automation ownership, and failure visibility.

## Creative and video

Current image and video generation tools, design systems, HeyGen, Hyperframes, programmatic renderers, media hosts, and available local creation skills may support production. AirOps and other content workflow systems may support research or production orchestration. Verify model capabilities, identity permissions, rights, disclosure, export, and cost at execution time.

## Integration layers

Existing MCP servers, application connectors, Composio-style integration layers, workflow automation, official SDKs, direct APIs, and CSV or warehouse exchange can all be valid. Choose the least privileged and most observable route that satisfies the operation.
