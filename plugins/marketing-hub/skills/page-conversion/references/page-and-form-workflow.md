# Page and form workflow

## Evidence

Inspect traffic and message match, conversion by step and segment, form errors and abandonment, recordings or heatmaps, polls, interviews, tickets, sales objections, speed, accessibility, broken paths, analytics integrity, lead quality, activation, refunds, and sales outcomes.

Label unavailable evidence. Do not infer rendered or interactive behavior from source markup alone.

## Page diagnosis

1. **Relevance:** intended person and situation are recognizable.
2. **Comprehension:** product, offer, value, and next step are understood quickly.
3. **Motivation:** desired progress matters in this moment.
4. **Credibility:** mechanism, specificity, terms, and proof support belief.
5. **Hierarchy:** the visual scan reveals the decision sequence.
6. **Objections:** fit, effort, price, switching, security, and risk are answered.
7. **Friction:** steps and choices demand only necessary work.
8. **Continuity:** source, page, form, confirmation, and follow-up agree.
9. **Quality:** the action produces a customer the system can support.

## Page types

- **Homepage:** category, audience, primary value, proof, and paths for different jobs.
- **Landing page:** one campaign promise, strict message match, focused action, complete objections.
- **Pricing page:** value metric, plan logic, limits, terms, comparison, and recommendation basis.
- **Feature page:** customer problem, workflow, result, proof, constraints, and related capabilities.
- **Content page:** useful answer, evidence, product relevance, and proportionate next step.

## Forms

For every field define why it is needed now, label, input type, autocomplete, validation timing, help, error focus, retained value, privacy explanation, consent, and server-side trust boundary. Cover interruption, duplicate submit, slow network, invalid file, unavailable service, and success recovery.

## Experiments

Prioritize hypotheses by evidence, expected customer impact, effort, reversibility, and learning value. Define control, treatment, population, assignment, primary outcome, practical threshold, quality guardrails, instrumentation, sample method, duration, and decision rule.

## Output

Return scope and evidence, ranked findings, observed defects, hypotheses and alternatives, complete recommended artifacts, implementation notes, experiment or rollout plan, measures, guardrails, and inaccessible behavior.
