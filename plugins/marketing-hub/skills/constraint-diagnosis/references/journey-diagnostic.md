# Journey diagnostic

## The journey

`reachable audience -> qualified attention -> understood value -> credible desire -> action -> activation -> retained value -> referral or expansion -> commercial return`

For a sales-led business insert lead capture, qualification, sales acceptance, and opportunity stages between action and activation. For a marketplace or multi-sided product diagnose each side separately.

For every transition record:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Volume | Eligible population entering the transition in the period |
| Rate | Share completing it, with numerator and denominator defined |
| Quality | Whether those completing it are the intended people or situations |
| Cost | Money, effort, or time spent to move them |
| Time | Elapsed time to complete the transition |
| Owner | Who can change it |
| Confidence | Verified, user-provided, analysis, hypothesis, or unknown |

Also inspect positioning, offer, proof, product readiness, support capacity, channel access, production capacity, lifecycle and sales handoffs, measurement, cash, and decision rights; they explain transitions that numbers alone do not.

## Failure patterns

- **Audience access:** the right people are not being reached. Owner skills: `$paid-media`, `$organic-social`, `$search-health`, `$creator-partnerships`, `$field-events`.
- **Qualification:** volume exists but the people or situations are wrong. Owner skills: `$truth-file`, `$outbound-prospecting`, `$page-copy`.
- **Comprehension:** people cannot quickly say what is offered or for whom. Owner skills: `$positioning-and-offer`, `$page-copy`.
- **Credibility:** the promise is understood but insufficiently supported. Owner skills: `$customer-evidence`, `$alternatives-map`, `$page-copy`.
- **Friction:** motivated users meet avoidable effort, uncertainty, or technical failure. Owner skills: `$page-conversion`, `$activation-path`.
- **Activation:** acquisition works but users do not reach first value. Owner skill: `$activation-path`.
- **Retention:** initial value does not recur or the product loses to alternatives. Owner skill: `$retention-recovery`.
- **Monetization:** value is experienced but the paid step is mistimed or unclear. Owner skills: `$upgrade-moments`, `$pricing-and-packaging-and-packaging`.
- **Economics:** the path works but costs more than the value it creates. Owner skills: `$pricing-and-packaging-and-packaging`, `$credit-and-lift`, `$growth-plan`.
- **Measurement:** the apparent constraint cannot be trusted because the data is incomplete or misdefined. Owner skills: `$tracking-plan`, `$credit-and-lift`.

Check measurement first. A constraint diagnosed from a broken denominator is a hypothesis about the data, not about customers.

## Evidence standard

- Use the definitions in the truth file; do not mix cohorts, time windows, or metric definitions across transitions.
- State the competing explanation for the weakest transition and what observation would separate them.
- Distinguish a transition that is weak from one that is merely unmeasured.
- A single-period drop is a signal to investigate, not a diagnosis.

## Diagnosis record

```text
Period and scope:
Journey table: <transition, volume, rate, quality, cost, time, owner, confidence>
Weakest evidenced transition:
Failure pattern:
Competing explanations:
Evidence confidence and gaps:
Owner skill:
Confirming test or first artifact:
```

The record is the input to `$growth-plan`, `$tactic-portfolio`, and every specialist that acts on the constraint.
