# Profile workflow

## Choose depth

- **Quick scan:** category, audience, promise, price shape, main workflow, visible proof, and material distinction.
- **Standard profile:** full product, positioning, pricing, proof, distribution, strengths, constraints, and implications.
- **Deep profile:** hands-on product evidence, chronology, content and search footprint, buying process, customer experience sources, and change monitoring.

Do not use a deep format when access is too weak to support it.

## Capture raw evidence

Keep URLs, checked dates, quotations within copyright limits, screenshots or captures when permitted, structured observations, and failed-access notes. Separate raw evidence from conclusions so later updates do not require repeating every interpretation.

## Research sequence

1. Confirm entity, product, geography, and current operating status.
2. Map target audience, category, promised outcome, offer, and route to market.
3. Inspect core workflow, capabilities, limits, onboarding, integrations, and service model.
4. Verify price structure, value metric, contracts, trials, visible terms, and plan boundaries.
5. Inventory proof, customers, certifications, claims, content, search surfaces, and distribution.
6. Find evidence of strengths, trade-offs, complaints, switching risk, and customer fit.
7. Compare change over time through changelogs, releases, archived sources when permitted, and dated observations.

## Profile contract

```markdown
# <Competitor> profile

Checked: YYYY-MM-DD
Scope and access:

## Summary and target situation
## Positioning, message, and offer
## Product workflow and capabilities
## Pricing, packaging, and terms
## Onboarding and customer experience evidence
## Proof and audience signals
## Distribution, content, and search footprint
## Strengths and better-fit cases
## Trade-offs, risks, and unknowns
## Implications for our product
## Sources
```

For multiple competitors, normalize only facts that are genuinely comparable. Use customer-led criteria and retain plan, version, and geography caveats.

## Update behavior

Record what changed, source, old and new observation, strategic effect, and next review trigger. Do not overwrite prior conclusions without preserving the evidence that invalidated them.
