# Retention workflow

## Diagnose churn

Classify bad fit or vanished need, failed activation, weak recurring value, reliability or usability failure, support failure, budget or organizational change, competitive switch, contract issue, and payment failure. Cancellation reasons are noisy; verify against behavior and service history.

## Cancellation flow

1. Make cancellation discoverable and accessible.
2. Confirm plan, effective date, retained access, data consequences, refunds, and reactivation.
3. Ask only necessary diagnostic questions.
4. Offer assistance, product correction, pause, downgrade, education, migration, payment update, or an authorized concession only when it matches the cause.
5. Confirm final state and stop incompatible messages.

Define account, entitlement, invoice, subscription, refund, data-retention, and reactivation behavior. Test immediate and period-end cancellation, duplicate requests, billing delay, failed webhook, team ownership, and app-store-managed subscriptions.

## Proactive retention

Use transparent signals such as failure to activate, material usage decline, unresolved product defects, support escalation, expiring contract, missing stakeholder, or payment risk. Define signal freshness, false-positive cost, eligible intervention, owner, frequency, and stop condition.

Do not label a person "at risk" from sensitive proxies or opaque scores without a legitimate operational basis.

## Dunning

Define payment-failure states, processor-owned retries, notification cadence, grace access, duplicate suppression, secure payment update, recovery, final suspension, and data retention. Reconcile processor and entitlement state before messaging or retrying.

Messages state what failed, amount or plan where safe, access consequence, real deadline, secure resolution path, and support. Avoid phishing-like links and manufactured urgency.

## Win-back

Use only after a suitable interval and with valid communication eligibility. Reference the actual prior relationship, address the known reason where appropriate, explain meaningful change, and stop after conversion, opt-out, invalid contact, or attempt limit.

## Measurement

Track gross and net retention, logo and revenue churn, reason mix, activation and value signals, cancellation completion, intervention acceptance, retained value after the observation window, refunds, complaints, support cost, failed-payment recovery, and involuntary churn. Use holdouts when estimating incremental saves.

## Output

Return diagnosis, segments, cancellation and recovery flows, complete copy, policy and data dependencies, event plan, experiment or rollout, guardrails, and failure reconciliation.
