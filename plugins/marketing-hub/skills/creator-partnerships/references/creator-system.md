# Creator and influencer system

## Spectrum

- editorial influencer or expert with trusted audience access;
- creator hired for native production skill;
- customer advocate sharing genuine experience;
- ambassador in a longer relationship;
- affiliate compensated for attributable outcomes;
- employee or founder voice;
- synthetic or staged creative, explicitly labeled and rights-cleared.

One person may occupy several roles, but terms and disclosure must distinguish them.

## Find and vet

Define audience overlap, subject credibility, content quality, product relevance, values, geography, platform, brand and legal risk, engagement quality, prior sponsorship behavior, conflicts, production reliability, and cost. Review a current sample of work and audience responses.

Verify identity and contact route. Do not treat follower count, an audience-estimation tool, or screenshots supplied by a seller as sufficient evidence.

## Outreach

State the truthful reason for fit, campaign or relationship concept, expected audience value, product experience, compensation range or process, timing, disclosure, rights discussion, and low-friction next step. Personalization must concern their work.

## Deal structure

Define cash, gifting, commission, hybrid, usage license, exclusivity, whitelisting or account access, deliverables, draft and review boundary, revisions, required and prohibited claims, disclosure, publication window, performance reporting, content ownership, cancellation, make-goods, payment, and disputes.

Verify current law and platform rules for the relevant jurisdiction and channel.

## Creative brief

Include audience, situation, product truth, required experience, communication job, useful points, proof, forbidden claims, CTA, disclosure, format, technical specs, source assets, review owner, and creative freedom. Avoid word-for-word scripts except for regulated or exact factual language.

## UGC program

Define creator profile, sourcing, sample or paid trial, briefing, production batches, quality rubric, rights, disclosure, storage, naming, paid amplification, performance feedback, renewal, and removal. Distinguish customer-created content from paid performance and staged assets.

## Measurement

Track qualified reach where available, content completion, audience response, referral traffic, attributed and assisted outcomes, retained customer value, creative reuse, production cost, rights cost, complaints, brand risk, and incrementality where feasible.

## Output

Return program thesis, vetting rubric, candidate evidence, outreach, deal term sheet, creative brief, run-of-show, rights and disclosure matrix, measurement, and renew/stop decision.
