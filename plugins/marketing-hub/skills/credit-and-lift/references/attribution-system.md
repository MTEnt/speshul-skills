# Attribution system

## Interpretation modes

- first touch for original recorded acquisition under a defined persistence rule;
- last touch for the final recorded influence before conversion;
- linear, position-based, time-decay, or custom multi-touch for descriptive allocation;
- self-reported source for remembered influence and channels tracking misses;
- platform attribution for optimization inside that platform's current rules;
- cohort and blended payback for economic management;
- holdout, randomized lift, geo, or matched-market work for incrementality;
- marketing-mix modeling for aggregate planning when spend, history, variation, expertise, and validation support it.

Choose models according to the decision, not because one is called more advanced.

## Business-model fit

For self-serve, connect first-party anonymous and user state, purchase, refunds, and retained value. For sales-led B2B, preserve account, contact, buying group, opportunity, offline activity, and long lag. For ecommerce, handle order value, returns, repeat purchase, promotions, and marketplace gaps. For subscriptions, use cohort retention and payback. For local or offline, connect call, booking, location, sale, and source with clear identity limits.

## First-party capture

Record raw landing URL and permitted campaign parameters, referrer, timestamp, session or anonymous ID, consent, and first and recent valid touch under explicit rules. On identity creation, link permitted records without overwriting stronger evidence. Carry source through third-party scheduling or checkout using supported parameters or server callbacks, then reconcile booking or purchase to the original record.

Define duplicate, cross-device, cookie loss, private browsing, ad blockers, shared devices, bot traffic, invalid parameters, offline conversion, deletion, and retention.

## Reconcile reports

Compare conversion definition, event time versus processing time, time zone, identity, deduplication, lookback, click and view treatment, model, currency, tax, refunds, offline imports, latency, consent loss, cross-device, filters, and date cohort. Build a table of what each source is designed to answer.

## AI, dark social, and untracked influence

Use self-report, referral logs, sales notes, dedicated landing or offer paths, controlled experiments, and qualitative research to supplement missing click trails. Label uncertainty; do not invent precision for private sharing or AI-assisted discovery.
