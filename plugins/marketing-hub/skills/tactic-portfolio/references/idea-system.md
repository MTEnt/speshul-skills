# Idea system

## Mechanism library

Use these as prompts, not a checklist to execute in full.

### Capture existing demand

Problem and solution pages, comparison pages, integration pages, category directories, app listings, marketplaces, partner listings, high-intent search, retargeted evaluation, and sales-response content.

### Create and shape demand

Original research, category education, operator essays, teardown series, product demonstrations, workshops, benchmark explanations, decision guides, contrarian theses, and issue-led campaigns.

### Borrow trusted distribution

Co-marketing, integration partners, newsletters, podcasts, communities, associations, creators, resellers, affiliates, experts, local networks, and customer advocates.

### Build useful assets

Calculators, graders, generators, templates, checklists, datasets, APIs, open-source utilities, diagnostic tools, migration helpers, and interactive demos.

### Reduce buying risk

Live demos, sandboxes, sample outputs, implementation plans, security packages, proof libraries, reference calls, transparent comparisons, paid pilots, trials, and guarantees that operations can honor.

### Improve conversion

Message-match repair, pricing clarity, form reduction, signup recovery, assisted evaluation, role-specific paths, objection handling, chat or sales handoff, proof placement, and error-path repair.

### Accelerate activation

Sample data, templates, setup concierge, implementation sprints, role-based onboarding, progress checkpoints, triggered help, milestone education, and team-invite support.

### Sustain value

Usage coaching, health reviews, customer education, office hours, community support, product reminders tied to state, renewal preparation, expansion discovery, and success documentation.

### Encourage referral and sharing

Useful export branding, collaboration invitations, shareable results, customer stories, reciprocal referral value, partner commissions, ambassador programs, community recognition, and customer-to-peer education.

### Support sales

Account research, trigger-based prospecting, discovery tools, ROI models, champion kits, battlecards, demo stories, proposal systems, deal-risk reviews, and stale-pipeline recovery.

### Use product and ecosystem loops

Public artifacts, embedded sharing, user-created templates, integration discovery, marketplace participation, data network effects, contribution programs, developer examples, and customer extensions.

### Local and field motion

Workshops, dinners, meetups, association programs, speaking, sponsorships, local partnerships, direct-mail experiments, field demonstrations, and customer councils.

### Resource-constrained tactics

Founder-led interviews, manual concierge onboarding, design-partner programs, targeted teardown offers, customer-language rewrites, existing-content refresh, support-derived FAQs, sales-call repurposing, and one-partner pilots.

## Selection

Assess audience reach, constraint fit, product advantage, proof readiness, cost, team capacity, time to signal, reversibility, measurement, channel saturation, and trust risk. Use explicit judgment rather than hiding choices behind a precise score.

Build a portfolio with one primary bet, one supporting improvement, and at most one cheap exploratory option when capacity supports it.
