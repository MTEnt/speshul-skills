# Social asset blueprints

Adapt structure to the current platform, audience, and account evidence. Do not copy a creator's recognizable phrasing or assume a reported best practice still holds.

## Single post

Provide channel and job, opening, central point, evidence or example, product relationship where relevant, closing action or discussion prompt, final copy, media direction, source links, disclosure, and accessibility text. The opening and body must deliver the same promise.

## Thread or multi-post sequence

Give the sequence one thesis. Define the opener, ordered claims or steps, evidence placement, transitions, final synthesis, action, and standalone meaning for posts likely to be separated by the feed. Remove filler posts created only to increase count.

## Carousel or document

Return cover, frame-by-frame message and visual role, source note, continuity device, final action, caption, and alt text. Check that every frame advances the argument and remains readable at realistic mobile size.

## Short-form video

```text
Audience state and channel job:
Opening frame and line:
Beat / visual / spoken line / on-screen text / duration:
Demonstration or evidence:
Closing action:
Caption and subtitles:
Source assets, rights, and disclosure:
Current placement checks:
```

The premise should remain understandable without audio. Preserve enough reading time and do not manufacture a reaction, customer, or event.

## Repurposing packet

Start from the source thesis, strongest evidence, reusable examples, and audience questions. Produce channel-native assets with distinct jobs rather than cropped duplicates. Keep a provenance link from each derivative to the source and identify any claim that needs re-verification.

## Listening brief

Define monitored audience, accounts, topics, product and competitor terms, buying or problem signals, languages, geography, sources, exclusions, scoring, review window, and approved response behavior. Return the public URL, observed text or event, date, why it matters, confidence, and recommended human action.

Listening does not authorize scraping around access controls, mass replying, or posting. High engagement is not automatically high relevance.

## Calendar row

Track asset ID, audience, theme, channel job, format, thesis, source, owner, draft, review, rights, disclosure, publish window, current-spec check, approved action, URL after publication, primary measure, guardrail, and learning.
