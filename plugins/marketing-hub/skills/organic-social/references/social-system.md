# Social system

## Channel job

Choose reach among a known audience, teach a criterion, demonstrate a product, build trust, generate useful discussion, support a launch, recruit participants, perform customer research, or move qualified people to a next action. Avoid using every network for the same job.

## Platform fit

Inspect current audience behavior and official product guidance. Common patterns may include professional commentary and documents on LinkedIn, concise public conversation on X, visual and short-form discovery on Instagram and TikTok, community and local distribution on Facebook, and durable short or long video on YouTube. Treat these as hypotheses, not fixed rules.

## Content themes

Derive themes from customer questions, product evidence, operator experience, research, demonstrations, support, sales, and useful industry interpretation. For each theme define audience, tension, point of view, proof, suitable formats, product relationship, and source pipeline.

## Formats and hooks

- concise observation and consequence;
- problem, mechanism, demonstration, and action;
- specific story with change and learning;
- comparison using fair criteria;
- carousel or document with one decision per frame;
- short video with premise, evidence, progression, and payoff;
- thread when ordered depth is useful;
- live or recorded conversation when participation adds value.

Open with recognizable relevance, tension, evidence, visible action, or a useful promise. The remainder must fulfill the opening.

## Carousel contract

Specify cover promise, audience, frame sequence, one point and visual job per frame, proof, final action, caption, alt text, dimensions verified currently, and export naming. Preserve legibility at actual mobile size.

## Short-form video

Define hook, visual evidence, spoken and on-screen text, beat timing, captions, source assets, rights, sound, crop, CTA, and loop or ending. Do not hide the product or useful idea behind prolonged setup.

## Reverse engineering

Analyze public work for audience, premise, structure, pacing, evidence, visual device, CTA, comments, distribution, and likely production method. Reuse functional principles, not wording, footage, brand identity, or distinctive sequence.

## Repurposing

Preserve source thesis and evidence, then adapt context, structure, visual grammar, length, and interaction to the destination. Produce fewer meaningful derivatives instead of slicing every paragraph.

## Listening and engagement

Define monitored entities, topics, competitors, questions, risk terms, and sources. Capture permitted public content with URL, date, author or community context, relevance, uncertainty, and proposed action. Route support risk, qualified questions, relationship opportunities, research evidence, and noise differently.

Never infer private intent, harvest contacts, impersonate participants, or automate argumentative replies.

## Calendar and measurement

Track thesis, theme, job, source, asset, platform, owner, review, publication, distribution, result, reuse, and retirement. Measure qualified reach, completion or saves, meaningful replies, profile and site behavior, leads, activation, retention, or research signal according to the job.

## Output

Return channel thesis, themes, finished assets, media and disclosure notes, engagement or listening plan, calendar when requested, measurement, and learning cadence.
