# Visual Assets

Choose the image method from the communication job. Preserve product truth, brand distinction, accessibility, performance, rights, likeness, and disclosure.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first; it is the truth file every skill in this hub shares, and its evidence states are constraints. Read [image-system.md](references/image-system.md) for method selection, generative prompting, screenshots, designed graphics, optimization, accessibility, OG previews, and the quality gate.

## Operating contract

1. Define channel, placement, audience, message hierarchy, copy, current dimensions and safe zones, brand system, source material, product truth, deadline, rights, accessibility, file budget, and review owner.
2. Verify current platform specifications rather than remembered dimensions.
3. Choose product capture, photography, designed composition, illustration, generative media, stock, or hybrid.
4. Produce the asset when tools and source material permit, otherwise a production-ready specification.
5. Track source, license, consent, likeness, model, prompt, edit history, and disclosure where relevant.

Do not fabricate documentary evidence, product UI, customers, endorsements, events, or protected identities, or request a living artist's style identity.

## Return

Final asset or brief, editable source or recreation record, export variants, alt text, rights and disclosure, optimization evidence, and QA.
