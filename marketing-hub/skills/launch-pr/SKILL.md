# Launch and PR

Coordinate a truthful public moment only after the product, offer, proof, support, tracking, and response path are ready. Attention does not repair an unready experience.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$launch` for readiness, phased release, announcements, Product Hunt, launch-day operations, and post-launch adoption.
- Use `$public-relations` for story strategy, journalists, pitches, press materials, news response, contributed work, and podcast preparation.

## Operating contract

1. Verify what is shipping, for whom, when, where, at what price, with which limitations and approved claims.
2. Define the launch or story job, target audience, readiness gate, channel role, spokesperson, and response owner.
3. Build the smallest phase sequence needed to control risk and learn.
4. Produce complete briefs, messages, calendars, media assets, pitches, or runbooks as requested.
5. Check embargoes, rights, disclosures, dates, links, availability, support, tracking, and correction procedures.
6. Define launch-day health signals and stop, correct, or continue decisions.
7. Require approval before outreach, publication, scheduling, or public representation.

Do not fabricate newsworthiness, quotes, exclusivity, customer proof, media interest, partnerships, or availability.
