# Paid-media system

## Channel selection

- intent-driven search when useful demand can be expressed in queries and auction economics work;
- feed, display, or video when creative can identify or create relevant demand;
- professional networks when role, company, buying group, or account coordination justifies cost;
- retargeting only with consent, exclusions, meaningful audience states, and a distinct next message;
- account-based programs only when account selection, buying-group coverage, sales coordination, and account-level measurement exist.

## Campaign architecture

Define account and campaign purpose, objective, optimization event, audience or query grouping, exclusions, geography, schedule, budget, bid method, creative logic, destination, naming, owner, learning period, and stop rules.

Keep acquisition, retargeting, customer, brand, competitor, experimental, and market segments separate only when budget control, message, measurement, or governance requires it.

## Search playbook

Map query intent to ad group, keyword or current targeting mechanism, negatives, ad promise, landing message, and conversion. Review actual search terms, match behavior, auction conditions, quality, downstream outcome, and invalid traffic. Stabilize relevance and exclusions before expanding automation.

### Responsive search-ad output

When generating RSAs or their current equivalent, return campaign and ad-group context, landing page, target intent, prohibited claims, headline assets with character validation against current official limits, description assets, path fields, pinning rationale only when required, extensions or assets, and a compliance check. Mark every dynamic field and do not repeat near-identical assets.

## Meta and other feed platforms

Structure around business objective, reliable event, market, audience exclusions, creative concepts, placement compatibility, and budget. Verify current automation, audience expansion, placement, attribution, and campaign-setup behavior; do not preserve named platform eras as universal rules.

Use creative to qualify the intended situation. Separate concept tests from minor variants and monitor downstream quality, frequency, fatigue evidence, and delivery concentration.

## LinkedIn and B2B

Define role, seniority, function, industry, company, account, buying group, and exclusions from the actual ICP. Choose lead form or site destination based on data need, experience, consent, sales SLA, and measurement. Thought-leader or employee formats require identity permission and current platform eligibility.

## Retargeting

Segment by real state: evaluated content, product action, incomplete conversion, active opportunity, customer, excluded recent converter, and suppressed contact. Define window from decision cycle, frequency, sequence, and stop state. Avoid stalking-like repetition.

## Budget, bidding, and payback

Base spend on learning volume, event quality, expected value, gross margin, sales or fulfillment capacity, cash risk, and observation window. Define daily and total caps, change authority, emergency stops, and reconciliation.

Progress automated bidding only when the optimization event and volume are trustworthy. Evaluate cohort payback and retained value, not reported return alone.

## Conversion tracking

Specify event source, exact trigger, identity, consent, deduplication, browser and server paths, offline outcomes, value and currency, attribution window, QA, latency, and source of truth. Reconcile platform, analytics, CRM, billing, and warehouse definitions.

## Audit guardrails

Do not recommend a setting change without evidence, affected scope, expected mechanism, dependency, risk, and verification. Avoid changing budget, bid, audience, creative, and destination simultaneously when diagnosis matters.

## Output

Return findings or build hierarchy, platform and objective rationale, audiences or queries and exclusions, budget and bid boundaries, creative requirements, tracking gate, destination requirements, launch checklist, reporting view, and decision rules.
