# Paid-platform readiness

Verify every platform-specific control, limit, policy, and campaign name against current first-party documentation. This reference defines decisions that must be resolved; it intentionally does not freeze vendor UI labels.

## Universal gate

- accountable business, billing owner, authorized account access, market, currency, time zone, and naming convention;
- product eligibility, restricted-category review, claim evidence, disclosure, trademark, and asset rights;
- campaign objective tied to a business event, reliable destination, consent behavior, privacy notice, and follow-up capacity;
- browser and server event ownership, deduplication, value and currency, test traffic, offline outcomes where applicable, and reconciliation source;
- audience source and permission, geography, language, exclusions, customer suppression, internal-traffic handling, and retention limits;
- budget cap, bid authority, learning window, maximum acceptable loss, pacing monitor, stop condition, and escalation owner;
- asset coverage by current placement, crop and safe-zone checks, captions or alternatives, URL and tracking parameters, and preview review;
- pre-launch test, change log, dashboard, review cadence, incident contact, and rollback or pause authority.

## Intent and shopping systems

Resolve query model, match or targeting behavior, negatives, brand and competitor policy, geography, schedule, landing-message match, product-feed ownership, price and inventory freshness, merchant eligibility, asset coverage, search-term review, and invalid-traffic handling.

For automated or cross-network campaign types, state which controls are ceded, what input quality is required, how brand and geography are constrained, and how incremental value will be tested.

## Feed, social, and video systems

Resolve business and ad identity, page or profile permission, domain verification where required, event priority, placement compatibility, audience expansion, customer-list authorization, lead-form data notice and routing, comments or moderation ownership, frequency observation, and creative-fatigue rules.

Identity-linked or employee-sponsored formats require the person's informed permission. Creator content requires the applicable paid-partnership disclosure and usage rights.

## Professional and account-based systems

Define account universe, buying roles, matched-audience source, coverage threshold, sales owner, CRM member status, lead or site destination, response SLA, opportunity exclusions, account-level reporting, and the point at which insufficient audience scale invalidates the plan.

## Audit record

For every recommendation capture observed evidence, confidence, affected entities, expected mechanism, prerequisites, cost and risk, exact authorized action, verification window, and revert or pause rule. Separate factual defects from optimization hypotheses.
