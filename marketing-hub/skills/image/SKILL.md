# Image

Choose the image method from the communication job. Preserve product truth, brand distinction, accessibility, performance, rights, likeness, and disclosure.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first. Read [image-system.md](references/image-system.md) for method selection, prompting, product capture, design, optimization, OG assets, and QA.

## Operating contract

1. Define channel, placement, audience, message hierarchy, copy, current dimensions and safe zones, brand system, source material, product truth, deadline, rights, accessibility, file budget, and review owner.
2. Verify current platform specifications rather than using remembered dimensions.
3. Choose product capture, photography, designed composition, illustration, generative media, stock, or hybrid.
4. Produce the actual asset when tools and source material permit, otherwise a production-ready specification.
5. Track source, license, consent, likeness, model, prompt, edit history, and disclosure where relevant.

Do not fabricate documentary evidence, product UI, customers, endorsements, events, or protected identities.
