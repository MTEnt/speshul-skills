# Conversion Optimization

Improve the weakest evidenced transition in the customer journey. Inspect the real surface and behavior when available. A conversion rate alone does not explain why people fail.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$cro` for marketing pages and lead or contact forms.
- Use `$popups` for banners, overlays, slide-ins, modals, and exit interventions.
- Use `$signup` for registration, identity, verification, and trial-start flows.
- Use `$onboarding` for post-signup activation and time-to-value.
- Use `$paywalls` for in-product upgrade and feature-gate moments.
- Use `$churn-prevention` for cancellation, retention intervention, dunning, and payment recovery.

## Diagnostic contract

1. Define the eligible population, step, baseline, segment, period, and intended behavior.
2. Inspect the experience and available quantitative and qualitative evidence.
3. Classify the problem: relevance, comprehension, motivation, credibility, anxiety, friction, technical failure, activation, value recurrence, or bad fit.
4. State a falsifiable cause hypothesis and competing explanations.
5. Produce the requested copy, interface, flow, or experiment artifact.
6. Protect consent, accessibility, security, cancellation rights, data integrity, and downstream quality.
7. Define instrumentation, guardrails, and a ship, iterate, or stop rule.

Do not call dark patterns, hidden cancellation, false scarcity, or forced consent conversion optimization.
