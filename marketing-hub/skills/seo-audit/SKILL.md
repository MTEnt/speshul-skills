# Seo Audit

Separate discovery, crawlability, rendering, canonical selection, indexing, ranking, traffic, and conversion. Evidence of one state does not prove the next.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first. Read [audit-system.md](references/audit-system.md) for the complete technical, international, page, content, and prioritization workflow.

## Operating contract

1. Define site, environments, markets, languages, important page types, business outcome, known changes, access, and reporting period.
2. Verify current search-engine guidance from official sources when behavior can change.
3. Inspect the actual site and representative rendered pages where possible.
4. Attach each finding to observed evidence, affected scope, likely impact, confidence, owner, fix, and verification.
5. Prioritize access and canonical defects before metadata polish.

Return the audit, implementation-ready fixes, validation steps, monitoring, and inaccessible or unverified areas. Do not guarantee rankings or indexation.
