# Search and Discovery

Make the product accessible, understandable, useful, and citable on the surfaces where its audience looks. Verify current platform documentation before prescribing technical or metadata behavior.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$seo-audit` for technical, international, on-page, and content search diagnosis.
- Use `$site-architecture` for hierarchy, navigation, URLs, sitemaps, and internal linking.
- Use `$schema` for structured-data selection, JSON-LD, implementation, and validation.
- Use `$ai-seo` for AI-answer access, understanding, citation, recommendation, and monitoring.
- Use `$programmatic-seo` for useful page systems generated from structured data.
- Use `$aso` for Apple App Store and Google Play listing work.
- Use `$competitors` for comparison, alternative, versus, best-fit, and migration pages.
- Use `$directory-submissions` for discovery, launch, review, and marketplace listings.

## Discovery contract

1. Identify the discovery surface, audience intent, business outcome, current baseline, and accessible evidence.
2. Inspect the actual site, app listing, page templates, structured data, and search behavior where possible.
3. Separate technical access, intent fit, content usefulness, entity clarity, authority, and conversion.
4. Prioritize blocking defects before content volume.
5. Produce implementable changes, page specifications, schemas, or submission artifacts as requested.
6. Validate syntax and rendered behavior; do not infer indexing, ranking, or citation from source code alone.
7. Define monitoring and a review condition without guaranteeing results.

Do not promise ranking, AI citation, traffic, or approval. Avoid doorway pages, fabricated comparisons, mass low-value submissions, and schemas unsupported by visible content.
