# Co-marketing system

## Partner identification

Look for audience overlap without direct message conflict, adjacent journey position, product or integration complementarity, trusted expertise, compatible quality and values, distribution access, operational capacity, and fair mutual benefit.

Disqualify unresolved reputation risk, weak audience relevance, one-sided work, conflicting claims, data misuse, hidden pay-to-play, and dependence on an unapproved relationship.

## Partnership forms

- joint research or benchmark;
- webinar, workshop, event, or course;
- guide, playbook, template, or tool;
- integration launch or workflow story;
- bundle, benefit, or reciprocal offer;
- newsletter, social, podcast, or community exchange;
- referral, affiliate, reseller, or channel arrangement;
- joint customer story with permission;
- sales enablement and account collaboration;
- contribution to an ecosystem or open project.

Choose by customer value, evidence, production capacity, distribution, rights, and measurement.

## Campaign design

Define premise, audience, useful output, partner roles, source evidence, message and claims, content and media, registration or access, distribution commitments, lead handling, consent, follow-up, support, budget, timeline, decision rights, and stop conditions.

## Outreach

Reference the real fit, propose a bounded customer outcome, describe contributions, identify timing, and ask for a specific exploration step. Avoid vague partnership requests and inflated audience claims.

## Operating agreement

Record owner per organization, scope, deliverables, review and approval, brand usage, IP and reuse, participant or lead data, privacy, costs and payment, disclosures, exclusivity, cancellation, incident handling, public claims, result sharing, and post-campaign asset ownership.

## Measurement

Use qualified participation, content use, partner-sourced or influenced behavior, product activation, pipeline or revenue with attribution limits, customer value, asset reuse, audience growth when relevant, relationship health, total cost, and operating burden.

## Output

Return partner criteria, candidate rationale, campaign concepts, selected brief, outreach, responsibility matrix, agreement checklist, calendar, distribution, lead handling, measurement, and renewal or exit rule.
