# Pricing system

## Start with a learnable price

When evidence is weak, choose a price and package that can be sold, delivered, measured, and changed safely. State the learning objective, target customer, economic floor, comparison context, sales feedback needed, review date, and maximum acceptable downside.

## Pricing models

Evaluate flat subscription, tiered subscription, per seat, active user, usage, transaction, asset or location, feature or edition, outcome-linked, license, one-time, service, and hybrid models. Check understandability, measurability, predictability, value alignment, cost alignment, abuse, procurement, expansion behavior, and customer control.

## Value metric

A useful metric tracks increasing customer value, is understandable before purchase, can be measured accurately, grows without punishing the desired behavior, and does not create surprising bills. Test edge cases, seasonality, shared use, unused capacity, fraud, and migration.

## Tier architecture

For each plan specify intended situation, core job, included capability, limits, service level, price, commitment, upgrade trigger, and why adjacent tiers remain distinct. Keep the core product useful. Avoid arbitrary feature withholding that makes the entry plan fail.

## Research methods

- interviews and sales calls for value and comparison logic;
- win/loss, discount, churn, expansion, and cohort data for actual behavior;
- competitor research for market context, not automatic matching;
- price-perception surveys for directional ranges with sample and hypothetical-bias warnings;
- MaxDiff or conjoint-style work for trade-offs when design expertise and sample permit;
- controlled package or price tests when identity, spillover, fairness, and volume permit;
- unit economics for margin, cost-to-serve, payback, and retained value.

Show method, sample, assumptions, confidence, and disagreement. Do not turn one survey threshold into an optimal-price claim.

## Pricing-page teardown

Inspect audience and plan fit, value metric, recommendation logic, included and excluded items, limit definitions, monthly and annual presentation, total cost, taxes and fees, trial and renewal, cancellation, comparison, proof, FAQs, accessibility, mobile behavior, CTA continuity, and machine readability. Verify current price and terms directly.

## Price increase

Define affected cohorts, contracts, rationale, notice, grandfathering or migration, billing implementation, support scripts, exceptions, cancellation and refund behavior, revenue and churn scenarios, data migration, rollback or correction, and monitoring. Test new, existing, and mixed customer states.

## Deliverable

Return evidence, pricing thesis, model and metric, tier table, economics, customer impact, page changes, research or experiment plan, migration and communications, risks, and review condition.
