# Referral and affiliate system

## Choose the mechanism

- natural sharing because the artifact, collaboration, or outcome is useful to another person;
- unprompted word of mouth supported by product value and customer language;
- customer referral with no reward or recognition;
- reciprocal product credit or benefit;
- cash or credit reward after a qualified event;
- affiliate commission for attributable promotion;
- ambassador relationship with broader participation;
- partner or reseller agreement with sales and service responsibilities.

Use the lightest mechanism that fits the relationship.

## Referral design

Define value moment, eligible advocate, invited-person fit, invitation channels, sender control, message, landing continuity, attribution window, qualifying activation or purchase, reward, caps, pending period, refund and reversal, fraud, support, privacy, disclosure, and taxes.

Product sharing should preserve the user's voice and never upload contacts or send without informed action.

## Viral mechanics

Consider collaboration invitations, shared outputs, public artifacts, templates, embedded attribution, network value, marketplace participation, and referrals. Model cycle time, branching, acceptance, activation, retention, and saturation. A viral coefficient estimate is a scenario until observed.

## Affiliate program

Specify application and approval, partner categories, commission basis, attribution and cookie or code logic, payout threshold and schedule, refunds, chargebacks, prohibited promotion, paid-search and trademark rules, coupon behavior, disclosure, creative, data access, reporting, fraud, audits, suspension, and termination.

## Communication

Write invitation, reminder, acceptance, progress, reward, rejection, expiry, fraud review, affiliate onboarding, and policy-change messages as applicable. Suppress after success, opt-out, invalid state, or attempt limit.

## Launch checklist

Verify economics, billing or ledger, terms, privacy, tracking, identity, duplicate behavior, rewards, taxes, support, abuse monitoring, copy, accessibility, analytics, staged rollout, reconciliation, and rollback or pause.

## Measurement

Track eligible advocates, referral action, accepted invitation, qualified activation, retained value, reward cost, fraud, complaint, support, affiliate quality, assisted outcomes, and incrementality using holdouts or phased comparisons where feasible.

## Output

Return mechanism decision, program specification, economics, terms checklist, complete messages, tracking and reward states, launch plan, fraud handling, measurement, and stop or scale condition.
