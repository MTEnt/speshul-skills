# AI discovery system

## Four distinct problems

1. **Access:** a relevant crawler or retrieval system can fetch allowed content.
2. **Understanding:** entities, products, people, facts, relationships, dates, and sources are unambiguous.
3. **Citation:** the content contains specific, useful, supportable material worth referencing.
4. **Recommendation:** product fit, independent evidence, constraints, and comparison context support inclusion for a real request.

Do not assume every system uses the same index, crawler, sources, freshness, or control files.

## Visibility audit

Inventory important questions and run repeatable observations across the actual target surfaces where permitted. Record prompt, model or product, mode, date, locale, answer, cited URLs, product mentions, fit accuracy, omissions, and volatility. A single answer is not a stable baseline.

Inspect robots and crawler policy, rendered access, canonical pages, source authority, entity consistency, third-party evidence, and whether important facts are available without hidden interaction.

## Content likely to be useful

Direct answers, current documentation, transparent pricing and availability, definitions, original data with methods, calculations, comparisons with fair criteria, implementation guides, limitations, change histories, expert authorship, and clear tables can support retrieval and citation when genuinely useful.

Use clear headings, self-contained passages, source and update dates, stable URLs, and correction paths. Preserve human value.

## Citations versus recommendations

Citation may reward a source that explains a fact. Recommendation additionally depends on fit, reputation, availability, alternatives, and evidence outside the vendor's own claims. Track them separately.

## Content by surface

- pricing: plans, value metric, limits, total commitment, date, and change ownership;
- product and docs: working behavior, prerequisites, examples, constraints, and version;
- comparison: neutral criteria, source dates, competitor strengths, and switching conditions;
- research: methodology, sample, limitations, downloadable data where appropriate, and correction;
- video and audio: descriptive pages, transcripts, chapters, titles, speakers, and source links;
- local and marketplace: entity, geography, availability, verification, and current listings.

## Agent readiness

Assess machine-readable navigation, authentication boundaries, stable action descriptions, explicit inputs and outputs, safety and confirmation, error states, and current documentation. Verify emerging standards before implementation.

Files such as `llms.txt`, extended knowledge bundles, or agent-action protocols are optional experiments unless current target-system evidence supports them. They do not replace crawlable, accurate web content.

## Monitoring

Track target question, source page, content change, crawl accessibility, observed mention, citation, recommendation accuracy, competitor presence, referral or self-reported influence, and checked date. Preserve volatility and attribution limits.

## Output

Return question set, visibility evidence, access and entity findings, content and evidence gaps, page specifications, optional machine-readable assets with support rationale, monitoring design, and no-guarantee statement.
