# Programmatic search system

## Playbook families

1. location and service-location pages with verified availability and local value;
2. integration pages built from real compatibility and workflow data;
3. product, plan, or competitor comparisons with sourced criteria;
4. use-case, role, or industry pages with materially different workflow evidence;
5. templates, examples, or galleries containing useful artifacts;
6. glossary and entity pages tied to a coherent knowledge model;
7. calculators, benchmarks, statistics, or data explorers;
8. directory, marketplace, provider, or listing pages with maintained records;
9. product or technology compatibility pages;
10. jobs, events, grants, regulations, or other time-bounded databases;
11. alternatives and migration paths with fair fit criteria;
12. question, problem, or workflow pages generated from validated support or product data.

## Selection test

For a sample page, explain the unique audience need, unique input data, useful output, source, update rule, and why the page should exist independently. Reject the family when the only variable is a keyword token.

## Data contract

Define entity IDs, field types, source, owner, freshness, required and optional fields, validation, relationships, localization, permissions, sensitive data, deletion, duplicate matching, and stale or unavailable behavior.

## Template contract

Specify title and heading logic, introductory answer, unique data, calculation or comparison, evidence, related entities, visual or interactive utility, product relationship, CTA, metadata, structured data, breadcrumbs, and accessibility. Avoid repeated filler paragraphs.

## URL and indexing

Define stable path, slug collision, canonical, pagination, filters, parameter handling, noindex and exclusion rules, sitemap inclusion, internal discovery, orphan prevention, redirects, and retirement. Prevent indexation when data is too sparse, duplicate, expired, unsafe, or unavailable.

## Quality checks

Automate schema, required fields, duplicates, broken links, invalid combinations, factual constraints, empty sections, performance, accessibility, and metadata. Manually review representative normal, sparse, extreme, duplicate, localized, and error cases against source data.

## Rollout and measurement

Release a bounded cohort, monitor crawl, indexation, query fit, engagement, conversion, data defects, support burden, and template regressions. Separate page-count growth from useful search and customer outcomes.

## Output

Return playbook choice, intent map, data schema, template specification, sample pages, index rules, internal linking, validation, rollout, monitoring, ownership, and retirement.
