# Marketing Strategy

Turn business goals and market evidence into choices. A strategy must say what will be prioritized, what will be deferred, why the bet is credible, and what evidence changes the decision.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$marketing-plan` for coordinated GTM, growth, fractional-CMO, budget, team, and operating plans.
- Use `$offers` for the complete commercial exchange, risk allocation, bonuses, guarantees, and truthful urgency.
- Use `$pricing` for price level, value metric, packaging, tiers, research, increases, and pricing-page decisions.
- Use `$marketing-ideas` for an evidence-ranked mechanism portfolio.
- Use `$marketing-psychology` for ethical behavioral and decision analysis.
- Use `$marketing-council` for structured disagreement across documented strategic lenses.

## Strategy contract

1. Verify the business outcome, baseline, audience, product truth, offer, proof, capacity, and time horizon.
2. Diagnose the current constraint before selecting tactics.
3. Compare a small set of plausible strategic choices, including the status quo.
4. Select one primary thesis and explain the sacrifice it requires.
5. Translate the thesis into now, next, and later work with owners, dependencies, metrics, and stop conditions.
6. Label frameworks, scores, and expert viewpoints as decision aids. They are not market evidence.
7. Return a usable plan or decision artifact, not only advice.

Do not produce an exhaustive plan when the user needs one decision. Do not use fake precision, invented proof, or a simulated expert voice as authority.
