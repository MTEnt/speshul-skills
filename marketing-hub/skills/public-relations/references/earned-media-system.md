# Earned-media system

## PR mix

Use owned press information, targeted journalist pitching, data or expert commentary, news response, contributed work, podcast or broadcast appearances, and relationship maintenance according to objective and evidence. Paid placement and sponsorship must not be represented as earned coverage.

## Story angles

Potential value may come from verified news, original data with method, consequential customer or market change, credible expert access, local relevance, a useful contrarian interpretation, public-interest impact, a significant partnership with approval, or a demonstrated product development. Test whether the story matters without the company name.

For each angle state audience consequence, why now, factual claim, source, distinctive evidence or access, spokesperson, counterargument, limitation, visual or data asset, owned destination, and facts that cannot be disclosed.

## Outlet and journalist research

Find current beat, geography, format, audience, recent relevant work, stated pitch preferences, employment or contributor status, conflicts, deadline behavior, and permitted contact route. Use specific work as the relevance basis. Do not scrape or mass-message generic lists.

## Pitch

Write an accurate subject, immediate relevance, actual news or useful idea, supporting evidence, source or asset availability, timing, and low-friction response. Keep company background secondary. Define one or two respectful follow-ups based on context and suppress after decline or opt-out.

## Press release or announcement

Use a factual headline and date, concise news, audience consequence, supporting detail, approved attributable quotations only, availability and limitations, company facts, contact, sources, and corrections. A release does not need inflated adjectives.

## Press page and media kit

Include current company and product facts, leadership or spokesperson bios, approved logos and images, usage rights, fact sheet, announcements, product captures, data or methodology where relevant, press contact, and reviewed date. Remove stale assets and claims.

## News response and press requests

Verify event, deadline, outlet and requester identity, exact question, expertise, disclosure, source restrictions, embargo, and approval. Answer only within evidence and experience. Preserve the question and final submitted response.

## Podcast preparation

Research show, host, audience, format, recent episodes, expected themes, recording and reuse rights, conflicts, and product relevance. Prepare a few evidence-backed ideas, stories, examples, likely questions, sensitive boundaries, concise bio, pronunciation, setup checklist, and follow-up assets. Do not script every sentence.

## Measurement

Track publication accuracy, target-audience relevance, referral behavior, qualified conversations, backlinks as secondary evidence, asset reuse, relationships, corrections, and reputation risk. Estimated impressions alone are weak evidence.

## Output

Return PR decision, story angles, evidence, target list and rationale, complete pitch or response, press material, spokesperson brief, follow-up rules, risks, approvals, correction path, and measurement.
