# Cold-email system

## Legitimacy and account safety

Verify current applicable law, sender and provider rules, identity, physical or legal identification where required, contact source, suppression, opt-out, domain authentication, sending infrastructure, volume, bounce handling, complaint response, data retention, and high-risk categories. Business contact details do not authorize unlimited automated outreach.

## Qualification

Require ICP and disqualifier match, observable company or role evidence, relevant trigger or situation, plausible value, correct role, verified contact route, source, checked date, confidence, and next research need. Separate fit from timing and access.

## Personalization

Use company or role context, recent operational signal, product or workflow evidence, relevant public statement, mutual context only when real, or an informed problem hypothesis. State uncertainty. Do not use sensitive inference or irrelevant personal details.

## Message frameworks

- observed situation -> plausible consequence -> relevant mechanism -> question;
- trigger -> why it may matter -> proof or example -> permission to continue;
- problem hypothesis -> disconfirming question -> useful resource or next step;
- referral or correct-owner request when the recipient's role is uncertain;
- concise breakup or close-the-loop message after a bounded sequence.

## Subject lines

Represent the actual message in plain language. Use account, problem, project, or question context where useful. Avoid fake reply or forward prefixes, deceptive urgency, symbols designed only to attract attention, and unrelated curiosity.

## Follow-up sequence

Each follow-up should clarify the hypothesis, add evidence, answer a likely risk, share a relevant resource, or close the loop. Define spacing from audience context and channel norms, maximum attempts, reply detection, other-channel coordination, and immediate suppression.

## Reply routing

Handle positive interest, question, referral, not now, wrong person, objection, opt-out, complaint, bounce, and automated response. Preserve owner, context, promised follow-up, and suppression. Never automate an argumentative response to objection or complaint.

## Measurement

Use delivered valid contacts, qualified replies, meetings held, opportunities, customer value, referrals, complaints, bounces, opt-outs, and domain health. Opens are unreliable and should not be the primary outcome. Public benchmarks are dated context, not a promise.

## Output

Return qualification and data rules, complete sequence, subject options where useful, personalization fields and fallbacks, reply routing, compliance and suppression, QA, measurement, and send-approval boundary.
