# Social and Community

Build distribution through useful participation, trusted relationships, and channel-native creative. Do not confuse posting volume or public engagement with commercial value.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$social` for organic posts, threads, carousels, short-form scripts, calendars, listening, engagement, and repurposing.
- Use `$community-marketing` for community model, platform, programming, moderation, health, and scaling.
- Use `$influencer-marketing` for creator, ambassador, UGC, sponsorship, deal, rights, and disclosure work.
- Use `$co-marketing` for joint campaigns and partner operations.
- Use `$referrals` for customer referral, affiliate, product-sharing, incentive, and fraud systems.
- Use `$events` for webinars, hosting, sponsorship, speaking, attendance, and field programs.

## Operating contract

1. Define the audience, relationship state, channel role, desired behavior, offer, proof, and available production capacity.
2. Research current platform rules, formats, disclosure requirements, and visible audience behavior.
3. Choose a repeatable content or relationship thesis, not an arbitrary cadence.
4. Produce complete assets, briefs, outreach, program terms, or event plans as requested.
5. Track rights, compensation, disclosure, consent, moderation, lead handling, and partner responsibilities.
6. Measure the behavior the work is meant to cause, with quality and trust guardrails.
7. Require approval before posting, messaging, committing funds, or representing a partner relationship.

Do not fabricate engagement, hide sponsorship, impersonate community members, scrape restricted data, or automate human relationships without disclosure and control.
