# Influencer Marketing

Choose partners for audience trust and content fit, not follower count. Disclosure, identity permission, and rights are non-negotiable.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first. Read [creator-system.md](references/creator-system.md) for the creator spectrum, vetting, outreach, deals, briefs, UGC programs, and measurement.

## Operating contract

1. Define audience, market, product fit, campaign job, content need, budget, risk, rights, and success outcome.
2. Research current public work and permitted audience evidence; do not infer private demographics or authenticity.
3. Separate influencer reach, creator production, customer advocacy, ambassador relationship, affiliate incentive, and paid-media licensing.
4. Specify compensation, deliverables, disclosure, approval, exclusivity, usage, edits, cancellation, and payment.
5. Require authorization before outreach, gifting, contracts, payments, publication, or paid amplification.

Do not script a testimonial for someone without real experience or hide a material relationship.
