# Content system

## Content jobs

Capture existing demand, define a problem, teach a criterion, demonstrate a mechanism, answer implementation questions, reduce risk, equip a champion, support activation or retention, present evidence, or create a source asset for distribution.

Each proposed piece must name its job and next useful behavior.

## Searchable and shareable

Searchable work satisfies known intent with clear usefulness, current evidence, accessible structure, and a proportionate product path. Shareable work carries a distinct idea, story, tool, demonstration, data set, or interpretation worth transmitting. Some assets do both; do not assume one format automatically does.

## Themes and clusters

For each theme define audience, situation, business relevance, distinctive source material, demand type, proof, formats, related product surfaces, internal-link role, and update owner. Create clusters only when pieces share a real learning path or decision relationship.

Map topics across problem recognition, category education, comparison, purchase, implementation, adoption, expansion, and advocacy where relevant. Do not force every topic through a funnel stage.

## Formats

Select article, guide, report, documentation, case analysis, video, webinar, newsletter, course, template, tool, calculator, glossary, comparison, landing page, social series, podcast, or event content according to the communication job and available evidence.

## Prioritization

Compare customer value, business relevance, evidence advantage, distribution access, search or sharing potential, production cost, dependency, freshness burden, time to signal, and reuse. Scores remain judgment; show why the top choice wins.

## Distribution and reuse

Choose search, newsletter, social, community, partner, sales, paid, product, event, and PR distribution based on actual access. Adapt the idea to each surface; do not mechanically divide one article into contextless fragments.

## CMS and content model

When implementation is in scope, define content types, fields, validation, references, slugs, localization, authorship, review state, scheduled publication, preview, media, structured data, redirects, permissions, revision history, API consumers, and migration. Keep display concerns out of the content model unless editors genuinely control them.

## Editorial governance

Track idea, job, audience, source, owner, status, review, publication, distribution, measure, parent asset, refresh trigger, and retirement. Define legal, expert, brand, accessibility, and factual review gates only where required.

## Quality gate

The work remains useful without its CTA, contains non-generic evidence or reasoning, has verified current facts, can be produced at the planned rate, and has a measure matching its job.
