# App-store audit

## Phase 1: identify and fetch

Confirm Apple App Store or Google Play URL, app and developer identity, package or bundle where available, countries and languages, device families, category, price, in-app purchases or subscriptions, latest version, ratings, review volume, update date, and visible metadata and assets.

Use current official references, including Apple App Store Connect help and Google Play Console help. Do not preserve remembered character counts or image sizes as timeless facts.

## Phase 2: maturity

Classify pre-launch, early listing, established with limited data, or mature with experiment and cohort evidence. The audit depth and confidence must follow available installs, listing visitors, conversion, activation, retention, ratings, reviews, and experiment history.

## Phase 3: score dimensions

Use a transparent qualitative or 0-5 scale with evidence for:

- audience and value comprehension;
- category and search-intent fit;
- name, subtitle or short description, and keyword use where applicable;
- long-description usefulness and readability;
- icon distinctiveness at search-result size;
- screenshot sequence, captions, product truth, and localization;
- preview video job and accessibility;
- rating quality, review themes, response, and prompt behavior;
- release notes, freshness, developer trust, privacy, and support;
- conversion continuity from acquisition promise through activation.

Do not combine dimensions into a precise overall forecast.

## Competitor comparison

Choose competitors by category, audience, query, alternative, and visible ranking context. Compare current listing message, asset sequence, proof, rating context, price presentation, localization, and update behavior. Represent strengths honestly and distinguish store presentation from product quality.

## Platform handling

For Apple, verify current app-information and version-information fields, keyword behavior by locale, screenshot and preview requirements by device, in-app event or product presentation, privacy details, review rules, and product-page testing availability.

For Google Play, verify current main and custom store listing fields, graphic and video assets, category and tags, data-safety and content declarations, localization, store-listing experiments, and metadata policy.

## Report

```markdown
# Store listing audit

## Scope, market, version, and evidence
## Performance baseline and data limits
## Dimension findings and scores
## Search and competitor context
## Metadata rewrite
## Screenshot and preview storyboard
## Rating, review, and release-note work
## Localization and policy dependencies
## Experiment plan and measurement
## Prioritized implementation
```

Track listing view-to-install, qualified install, activation, retention, rating, review quality, and support burden. Treat third-party benchmark ranges as contextual and dated.
