# Aso

Verify live listing and official store rules before scoring. Field limits, assets, policies, and experiment features can change.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first. Read [store-audit.md](references/store-audit.md) for store retrieval, maturity, scoring, competitors, platform handling, and report output.

## Operating contract

1. Confirm store, app identity, countries, devices, category, audience, product version, and business outcome.
2. Inspect the current live listing and available store-console performance evidence.
3. Verify current first-party field, asset, privacy, review, localization, and experiment requirements.
4. Score observations with evidence and label inaccessible console-only information.
5. Produce complete metadata and screenshot narrative from capabilities available in the submitted build.

Do not show unavailable features, manipulate reviews, stuff keywords, or treat public benchmarks as universal targets.
