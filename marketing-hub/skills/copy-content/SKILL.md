# Copy and Content

Produce useful, specific material from product truth, customer evidence, and a defined audience state. Do not use polished language to conceal a weak offer or unsupported claim.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$copywriting` for new or substantially rewritten page and campaign copy.
- Use `$copy-editing` for review, tightening, proofreading, and factual or strategic refreshes.
- Use `$content-strategy` for content portfolios, topics, briefs, distribution, governance, and CMS requirements.
- Use `$lead-magnets` for complete downloadable resources and their delivery journeys.
- Use `$free-tools` for calculators, graders, generators, validators, demos, and other interactive utility.

## Production contract

1. Define the audience, situation, job, stage, channel, desired action, and source evidence.
2. Inspect the product and offer before writing claims.
3. Choose one message hierarchy or content thesis before generating variants.
4. Write the complete requested artifact in the real format and length.
5. Annotate assumptions or claim dependencies only where the user needs them for review.
6. Check specificity, voice, proof proximity, information scent, accessibility, and channel fit.
7. Provide alternatives only when they represent meaningfully different strategic choices.

No placeholder copy, fake testimonials, fake numbers, keyword stuffing, bulk derivatives from weak source material, or generic CTA language when a specific action can be named.
