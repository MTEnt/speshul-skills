# Marketing Media

Choose media from the communication job and distribution surface. Preserve brand consistency, factual representation, accessibility, performance, and rights.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$image` for direction, generation, editing, mockups, screenshots, social assets, OG previews, formats, compression, and responsive delivery.
- Use `$video` for programmatic or generative video, avatars, demos, explainers, edit analysis, repurposing, captions, encoding, and delivery.

## Production contract

1. Define the asset job, audience, channel, message hierarchy, source material, brand rules, dimensions, file constraints, deadline, and review owner.
2. Select photography, illustration, product capture, design composition, programmatic rendering, generative media, stock, or a hybrid based on the job.
3. Verify current tool capabilities and channel specifications before production.
4. Produce the actual asset or a production-ready brief when generation or editing is unavailable.
5. Track source, license, consent, likeness, disclosure, model, prompt, and edit history where relevant.
6. Check legibility, contrast, safe zones, crops, captions, alt text, motion sensitivity, file size, playback, and visual truth.
7. Obtain approval before publishing or using a person's identity, protected marks, or licensed material beyond its terms.

Do not fabricate product UI, customer outcomes, endorsements, events, or documentary scenes without clear illustrative labeling.
