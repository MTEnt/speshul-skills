# Lifecycle email system

## Journey model

For each message define eligibility, trigger, known context, job, CTA, success event, delay rationale, exclusion, stop condition, fallback data, and next state. Prevent duplicate journeys and contradictory messages.

## Sequence families

- subscriber welcome and expectation setting;
- lead-magnet delivery and related help;
- trial or account activation;
- incomplete setup recovery;
- education tied to recurring product value;
- evaluation, nurture, and objection handling;
- launch, availability, and release adoption;
- newsletter with a stable reader promise;
- transaction and service notification;
- usage milestone, renewal, expansion, and customer education;
- inactivity, churn-risk, payment, cancellation, win-back, and reactivation.

Transactional messages prioritize required account or transaction information over promotion.

## Message structure

Include recognizable sender, accurate subject, useful preview, opening context, one central idea, relevant evidence or instruction, CTA and destination expectation, and required identity, preference, or unsubscribe controls.

Use personalization only when the data is reliable and changes usefulness. Provide safe fallbacks for missing or stale fields.

## Copy principles

State why the message is relevant now, show the useful action, keep proof near claims, and make deadlines real. Avoid hiding promotional intent, false urgency, vague click bait, unnecessary biography, and unsupported benefits.

## Timing and frequency

Set delays from customer behavior, product cadence, decision cycle, urgency, quiet expectations, and other active journeys. Define global and journey-level frequency, priority, collision, and suppression.

## Deliverability and compliance

Verify domain authentication, sender reputation, list source, consent or lawful basis, identification, unsubscribe, bounce and complaint handling, suppression, volume changes, tracking, retention, and current jurisdiction and provider rules. Separate necessary service communication from marketing.

## QA

Test variables, fallback, segmentation, eligibility, links, redirects, tracking, plain text, responsive rendering, dark mode where relevant, accessibility, localization, time zones, preference center, unsubscribe, reply handling, duplicate triggers, conversion suppression, provider retry, and outage behavior.

## Output

Return journey diagram or table, full sequence, trigger and timing, data fields, segmentation, suppression and failure handling, deliverability and compliance requirements, QA, primary measure, guardrails, and activation approval.
