# Context workflow

## Evidence states

- `verified`: inspected in a current authoritative source;
- `user-provided`: supplied by the user but not independently checked;
- `analysis`: derived from identified evidence;
- `hypothesis`: plausible and testable, but not established;
- `unknown`: required information is unavailable.

Add sources and checked dates when facts can drift. Preserve customer wording only with its origin and use boundary.

## Context sections

Capture only fields that change decisions:

1. Business model, commercial objective, stage, geography, capacity, and current constraint.
2. Product availability, working behavior, price, terms, prerequisites, limitations, and roadmap claims that may not be public.
3. Users, buyers, champions, approvers, blockers, and beneficiaries as distinct roles when necessary.
4. Triggering situation, desired progress, current workaround, urgency, cost, and switching risk.
5. Direct products, alternative categories, services, internal processes, and inaction.
6. Category frame, differentiated capability, resulting value, reason to believe, and bad-fit cases.
7. Offer, commitment, customer effort, delivery scope, exclusions, and risk treatment.
8. Sourced language, objections, proof inventory, approved claims, voice rules, and prohibited claims.
9. Primary customer behavior, commercial measure, baseline definitions, data gaps, and operating constraints.

Do not merge a buying group into one fictional persona. Exclude decorative demographics or personality traits that do not change product, message, channel, or sales behavior.

## Document contract

```markdown
# Marketing Context

Last reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD
Evidence scope: <sources and period>

## Business and product
## Audience and buying situation
## Customer progress and alternatives
## Positioning, offer, and bad fit
## Customer language and objections
## Proof inventory and claim limits
## Voice and creative boundaries
## Measurement and operating constraints
## Unknowns and active hypotheses
## Revision history
```

Keep deep source material linked rather than pasted. Add a newest-first revision entry only for substantive changes.

## Completion test

A downstream worker can identify the audience, situation, offer, proof, voice, forbidden claims, and meaningful unknowns without asking the same foundational questions again.
