# Directory workflow

## Selection

Evaluate audience overlap, category fit, geography, editorial quality, current traffic or community evidence, search and AI-discovery presence, profile ownership, update ability, cost, data use, review integrity, support, and maintenance burden.

Classify product launch platforms, software directories, category marketplaces, review platforms, app or integration marketplaces, AI or agent registries, local and industry directories, open-source listings, and community-maintained lists. Verify each candidate currently.

## Core submission packet

Maintain verified product name, one-line description, short and long descriptions, category, use cases, audience, website, pricing and trial statement, founding or company facts only where approved, contact route, social and documentation links, logo, screenshots, video, launch date, makers or team, privacy and terms, and source date.

Create positioning variations by directory audience and field length without changing factual claims.

## Product Hunt or anchor launch

Use only when the platform audience and launch job align. Verify current rules, product eligibility, media fields, maker behavior, scheduling, discussion, and promotion policy. Prepare truthful copy and assets, team coverage, support, tracking, and follow-up. Never coordinate fake votes, reviews, or undisclosed incentives.

## Review platforms

Claim and maintain profiles through authorized routes. Invite eligible customers neutrally, follow current platform and incentive rules, disclose incentives where required, avoid cherry-picking that violates policy, and never write or script customer reviews. Route negative feedback to accountable support without pressuring removal.

## Destination strategy

Choose the best truthful destination: homepage, product page, integration, comparison, use-case, launch page, app listing, documentation, or localized page. Preserve campaign parameters and message continuity. Do not point every listing to an unrelated homepage merely for links.

## Tracker schema

```text
directory,audience,category,url,requirements,owner,cost,submitted_facts,asset_version,status,checked_date,submission_date,live_url,profile_access_reference,traffic_or_lead_signal,review_date,next_action
```

Keep credentials out of the tracker.

## Measurement and maintenance

Track approval, listing accuracy, referral quality, assisted discovery, qualified leads, reviews, profile engagement, link health, cost, and maintenance. Recheck material listings after product, price, brand, or policy changes. Remove or correct stale and low-trust listings where possible.

## Output

Return prioritized directory set with rationale, current requirements and sources, complete submission packet and variants, tracker, launch or review plan, destination mapping, approvals, and measurement.
