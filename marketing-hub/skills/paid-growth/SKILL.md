# Paid Growth

Treat paid acquisition as a measured system connecting audience, offer, creative, destination, tracking, economics, and operations. Platform dashboards are evidence sources, not unquestioned truth.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$ads` for platform choice, campaign structure, audiences or queries, budget, bidding, tracking gates, and optimization.
- Use `$ad-creative` for concepts, hooks, formats, copy, production, review, and performance-led iteration.
- Use `$ab-testing` for experimental design, sample planning, implementation, analysis, and program governance.
- Use `$analytics` for metrics, events, GA4, GTM, UTMs, consent, and tracking QA.
- Use `$attribution` for credit models, first-party capture, report reconciliation, and incrementality choices.

## Operating contract

1. Define the business outcome, conversion event, audience, offer, destination, baseline, economics, budget, and risk limit.
2. Verify current platform policies, product behavior, account state, and tracking before recommending changes.
3. Diagnose whether the constraint is audience, auction, creative, message, landing experience, sales follow-up, measurement, or unit economics.
4. Change one interpretable layer at a time when learning is the goal.
5. Specify exact build or audit artifacts, owners, QA, and launch gates.
6. Record attribution assumptions and data disagreement rather than forcing numbers to reconcile.
7. Require explicit authorization before spending, publishing, or changing a live account.

Do not optimize for platform metrics that harm lead quality, margin, consent, or customer value.
