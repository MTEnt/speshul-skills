# Launch system

## Readiness gate

Verify product behavior, eligible users, availability, geography, platform, price, billing, terms, migration, known limitations, reliability, support, documentation, onboarding, tracking, privacy, security, approved claims, proof, media, sales readiness, incident ownership, and correction path.

Block public claims whose product, policy, proof, destination, or support dependency is not ready.

## Launch brief

Define audience, triggering situation, desired behavior, product change, promise, mechanism, proof, constraints, launch job, readiness threshold, success and health signals, budget, owner, decision times, and what the launch is not trying to achieve.

## Phases

1. **Internal readiness:** team use, documentation, support, tracking, claims, and escalation.
2. **Controlled alpha:** narrow qualified participants, direct observation, fast issue handling, explicit expectations.
3. **Beta:** broader evidence against defined use, reliability, support, and activation thresholds.
4. **Early access:** qualified demand, capacity and eligibility rules, waitlist, expectation control.
5. **General availability:** broad distribution with stable product, operations, and correction.
6. **Adoption:** education, activation, proof, customer success, sales enablement, and continued learning.

Each phase needs entry, audience, promise, capacity, instrumentation, support, exit, and rollback or correction.

## Channel roles

Assign owned product, website, email, sales, partners, communities, social, earned media, events, directories, and paid distribution only where they serve the launch job. Produce message hierarchy, announcement copy, support and product content, lifecycle assets, sales material, media, tracking, and response templates.

## Product Hunt and launch platforms

Use only when audience and launch job align. Verify current eligibility, submission, maker, asset, discussion, voting, promotion, and scheduling rules. Prepare accurate product information, media, team coverage, support, destination, and follow-up. Never coordinate fake votes, reviews, comments, or undisclosed incentives.

## Launch-day operations

Create a timed run-of-show with release owner, product and infrastructure checks, link and tracking checks, publication order, support and incident channels, spokesperson, approval authority, known-issue wording, monitoring, decision points, correction, status updates, and stop conditions.

Track product health, qualified demand, activation, sales handling, support burden, sentiment with source, and analytics integrity. Vanity reach cannot override customer harm.

## Post-launch

Resolve incidents and unanswered questions, correct inaccurate content, support adoption, route qualified demand, gather evidence, update sales and help material, document learning, retire temporary assets, and decide continue, expand, reposition, or stop.

## Output

Return launch decision, readiness matrix, phase plan, audience and message, channel and asset map, timeline and owners, rights and approvals, run-of-show, measurement, incident and correction plan, and post-launch adoption work.
