# Community system

## Models

- peer support and problem solving;
- practitioner learning and professional identity;
- product education and implementation;
- contributor or open-source collaboration;
- research panel or customer council;
- customer advocacy and recognition;
- local, cohort, alumni, or event-based connection;
- marketplace participant or partner network.

State what members receive from each other that the host cannot provide alone.

## Platform choice

Compare forum, owned community, Slack, Discord, Facebook Group, subreddit, GitHub, product-embedded space, event platform, and email-based group by discoverability, persistence, synchronous behavior, identity, moderation, access, portability, privacy, analytics, cost, accessibility, and exit risk. Verify current platform policy and features.

## Phases

1. **Research:** validate member problem, interaction need, founding cohort, and host legitimacy.
2. **Seed:** invite a bounded group, establish norms, facilitate useful interactions, and learn the operating load.
3. **Repeat:** establish programming, member roles, knowledge capture, moderation, and onboarding.
4. **Scale:** distribute ownership, improve discovery and safety, segment only when needed, and protect quality.
5. **Mature or retire:** measure sustained value, archive knowledge, migrate or close responsibly when the model no longer works.

## Programming

Use office hours, peer review, expert sessions, showcases, challenges, working groups, local meetups, research discussions, release education, and member-led events only when they serve the community's value exchange.

## Governance and safety

Define membership, verification, privacy, code of conduct, commercial boundaries, moderation levels, warnings, appeals, bans, harassment and safety escalation, vulnerable-member handling, staff access, record retention, legal concerns, and incident communication.

## Health metrics

Track active useful contributors, first contribution, response quality and time, solved problems, repeat participation, member-to-member activity, retained cohorts, knowledge reuse, customer or product outcomes, moderation incidents, staff hours, and cost. Member count and message volume can mislead.

## Output

Return community thesis, member jobs, model and platform rationale, founding cohort, onboarding, norms, programming, roles, moderation and escalation, health dashboard, phase gates, budget, and retirement conditions.
