# Editing system

## Seven passes

1. **Truth:** product behavior, price, date, availability, proof, quotation, permissions, and compliance.
2. **Audience and offer:** intended person, situation, progress, value, commitment, and bad fit.
3. **Hierarchy:** what the reader learns first, section jobs, missing decisions, and action path.
4. **Credibility:** mechanism, specificity, evidence placement, caveats, and objection handling.
5. **Clarity:** concrete language, defined terms, active construction, referents, and sentence logic.
6. **Voice and rhythm:** vocabulary, tone, cadence, repetition, transitions, and unnecessary performance.
7. **Interface and correctness:** headings, labels, CTA, errors, metadata, mobile scan, accessibility, grammar, punctuation, and formatting.

Stop early for a quick pass only when the user requests it and the earlier passes reveal no material strategy or truth defect.

## Scoring

When a score helps comparison, grade truth, audience fit, clarity, specificity, proof, hierarchy, voice, action continuity, and accessibility separately. Explain evidence for low scores. Do not present subjective scoring as measured conversion potential.

## Common corrections

- replace vague benefit words with the concrete change and mechanism;
- remove throat-clearing and repeated conclusions;
- split stacked claims and attach evidence to each;
- resolve ambiguous pronouns and floating modifiers;
- convert nominalized processes into clear actions where voice allows;
- replace internal jargon with audience language, or define required technical terms;
- make lists parallel only when the items are genuinely comparable;
- remove filler transitions and let logical sequence carry the reader;
- name the actual CTA action and next state.

Plain language does not require short words at all costs. Prefer the term the audience understands accurately.

## Content refresh

Inventory claims, dates, links, screenshots, prices, product behavior, search intent, internal links, proof, metadata, and CTA. Classify keep, correct, expand, consolidate, redirect, archive, or remove. Preserve valuable URL history and migration behavior where relevant.

Re-research drift-prone facts from primary sources. Do not change publication dates merely to make old content look new.

## Output modes

- clean replacement copy;
- before/after excerpts with reason;
- line or section comments;
- prioritized issue list;
- claim-verification table;
- refresh brief with redirects and dependencies.

State whether changes are factual, strategic, structural, voice, or mechanical.
