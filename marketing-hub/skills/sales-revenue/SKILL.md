# Sales and Revenue

Connect marketing activity to qualified commercial work. Protect contact data, qualification integrity, sales capacity, and customer trust.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it before asking foundational product, audience, proof, or voice questions. Preserve its evidence states and surface stale or contradictory entries.

## Route the task

- Use `$prospecting` for account and contact discovery, current sources, demand signals, qualification, and list handoff.
- Use `$revops` for lifecycle, scoring, routing, SLAs, CRM automation, pipeline, deal desk, hygiene, and reporting.
- Use `$sales-enablement` for decks, one-pagers, objections, battlecards, ROI tools, demos, proposals, and playbooks.

## Operating contract

1. Define the commercial outcome, ICP, disqualifiers, evidence standard, owner, and capacity limit.
2. Verify contact and company facts from permitted current sources; record source and checked date.
3. Separate fit, intent, engagement, and readiness instead of collapsing them into one unexplained score.
4. Make lifecycle stages, entry and exit criteria, ownership, timing, and failure handling explicit.
5. Produce the requested lead sheet, routing rules, CRM specification, dashboard contract, or sales asset.
6. Test handoffs against duplicates, missing data, stale records, reassignment, opt-out, and no-response paths.
7. Require approval before enriching, importing, routing, or contacting real people.

Do not invent leads, emails, roles, intent, quotes, objections, or pipeline outcomes.
