# Marketing Ideas

Generate mechanisms that fit the business, not a generic wall of tactics.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first. Read [idea-system.md](references/idea-system.md) for the mechanism library, selection rules, and experiment-ready output.

## Operating contract

1. Identify the audience, problem, journey constraint, product advantage, proof, budget, capacity, and acceptable time to signal.
2. Generate across several relevant mechanism families before choosing channels.
3. For every candidate state audience, mechanism, prerequisite, artifact, effort or cost, signal, metric, risk, and reason it fits.
4. Reject ideas that require unavailable proof, access, capacity, permission, or economics.
5. Rank a small portfolio and show what evidence would change the order.

Return selected ideas, adversarial critique, first artifact or test, and deferred options. Do not publish, contact people, or spend without authorization.
