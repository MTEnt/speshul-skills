# Paywall workflow

## Trigger types

- trial start or end;
- value demonstrated and expansion offered;
- feature request from a lower plan;
- usage or capacity threshold;
- collaboration or governance requirement;
- premium output or export;
- plan comparison initiated by the user;
- temporary offer based on a real policy.

Avoid immediate paywalls before value when the model requires experience, repeated interruptions after dismissal, and prompts unrelated to the current task.

## Screen components

Explain current state, unlocked value, why it matters now, plan and recommendation basis, price and billing period, total commitment, trial or renewal, cancellation and refund terms, usage or entitlement limits, proof when relevant, primary purchase action, alternative plan or comparison, and safe return.

## Paywall patterns

Adapt hard gate, soft limit, metered usage, reverse trial, trial expiry, premium feature, team or governance upgrade, add-on, and expansion paywall. Define what remains available if the user declines.

## Upgrade flow

Cover plan selection, billing interval, tax or address, payment method, authentication, confirmation, entitlement update, proration, invoice, failed payment, duplicate submit, delayed webhook, cancellation during purchase, and rollback or reconciliation.

## Timing and frequency

Set eligibility, first exposure, repeat exposure, suppression, cooldown, cross-device state, and termination. Use real product state rather than a fixed calendar where possible.

## Experiments

Test timing, value explanation, plan recommendation, comparison, proof, commitment framing, trial structure, or interaction friction separately. Guard refunds, chargebacks, retained usage, support contacts, accessibility, and trust.

## Output

Return current-state evidence, paywall type and trigger, screen hierarchy and complete copy, entitlement and billing state diagram, analytics events, experiment or rollout, policy dependencies, QA, and stop conditions.
