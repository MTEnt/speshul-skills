# Operating model

Use this module for broad requests that connect research, strategy, production, and measurement. Skip it for a narrow deliverable with sufficient context.

## Work through decision gates

### Foundation gate

Required before strategy:

- the business outcome and time horizon are explicit;
- product and offer facts are separated from aspiration;
- the audience and buying situation are specific enough to exclude someone;
- current performance and data limitations are known when optimization is requested.

If these are missing, gather only what can change the immediate decision.

### Strategy gate

Required before production:

- one primary audience and buying situation are selected;
- the relevant alternative or status quo is named;
- the promised value follows from a real differentiated capability;
- proof and claim limits are known;
- the primary constraint and chosen route to market are explicit.

Do not write a large asset set while these decisions remain contradictory.

### Production gate

Required before execution:

- each asset has a job, audience state, channel, CTA, owner, and review condition;
- claims have usable proof or approved hedging;
- source, likeness, music, font, and generated-media rights are tracked when relevant;
- platform and format constraints are current.

### Learning gate

Required before calling work successful:

- the success metric, baseline, window, and decision threshold were defined before launch;
- instrumentation covers the intended path;
- observed results are distinguished from causal conclusions;
- the next action follows from evidence rather than post-hoc storytelling.

## Diagnose the constraint

Map the user journey in plain language:

`reachable audience -> qualified attention -> understood value -> credible desire -> action -> activation -> retained value -> commercial return`

Locate the weakest evidenced transition. Common failure patterns:

- **Audience access:** the right people are not being reached.
- **Qualification:** volume exists, but the people or situations are wrong.
- **Comprehension:** people cannot quickly explain what is offered or for whom.
- **Credibility:** the promise is understood but insufficiently supported.
- **Friction:** motivated users encounter avoidable effort, uncertainty, or technical failure.
- **Activation:** acquisition works but users do not reach first value.
- **Retention:** initial value does not recur or the product loses to alternatives.
- **Economics:** the path works but costs more than the value it creates.
- **Measurement:** the apparent constraint cannot be trusted because the data is incomplete or misdefined.

Choose work that addresses the current constraint. A full-funnel plan can show later stages, but only the current stage receives detailed immediate production unless dependencies require otherwise.

## Plan at three horizons

- **Now:** one decision or artifact that can remove the current constraint.
- **Next:** the follow-on work unlocked if the signal is positive.
- **Later:** explicitly deferred options; do not build them yet.

Every action needs an owner or responsible role, a completion condition, a signal, and a decision it informs. Dates belong only where scheduling is actually in scope.

## Hand off cleanly

Every module should receive:

- source context and evidence ledger;
- selected audience and buying situation;
- current constraint;
- offer and claim boundaries;
- requested artifact, channel, format, and approval state;
- metric and decision threshold.

Every module should return:

- the finished artifact or explicit decision;
- sources and assumptions used;
- unresolved dependencies;
- review or measurement condition;
- recommended next module only when needed.
