# Competitors

Help a buyer choose accurately. The home product does not need to win every criterion or audience.

If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first. Read [comparison-pages.md](references/comparison-pages.md) for formats, research, architecture, SEO, page templates, and quality rules.

## Operating contract

1. Define audience, comparison intent, products or alternatives, market, plan or version, and desired next action.
2. Research current first-party product, pricing, terms, documentation, and credible customer experience evidence.
3. Choose criteria from customer decisions, not the home product's strongest features.
4. Cite dates and sources for drift-prone claims and mark unknowns.
5. Write the complete useful page, including where the competitor is a better fit.

Do not fabricate deficiencies, imply affiliation, create trademark confusion, or build a table designed only to force a predetermined winner.
